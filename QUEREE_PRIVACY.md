# Quer.ee Extension Privacy

Using the extension does not require sharing data with Quer.ee.

## Background Page Visits

Quer.ee opens Google/Bing result websites for inspection, including results you
do not click. Those sites receive your IP address and can run page scripts.

Filter-list updates contact their providers, and Android's background check
contacts Quer.ee. These requests expose your IP address, not your searches.

Desktop inspections do not use your ordinary cookies or logins. Android
inspections share cookies and logins with your other private tabs, not normal
tabs. If logged in there, inspected pages and contributed text can contain
personalized information. Private searches
themselves are not inspected or contributed.

Inspection visits may appear in history or be visible to other extensions.
Desktop history cleanup tries to remove only inspection visits, but a
simultaneous visit to the same address could also be removed. History changes
may sync to other devices.

Google and Bing can read Quer.ee's labels and personal category names displayed
on their pages.

## Optional Contribution

Contribution is off by default and requires an invitation key and your
permission. It shares result addresses (including redirect destinations), titles,
descriptions, limited page text and reports about the checks, including results
you did not click. Addresses and text can contain identifying information.
The extension also asks Quer.ee for earlier
reports about those addresses.

Accepted pages become publicly searchable. Other invited contributors can look
up an address and see its observations, inspection time and exclusion reasons,
but not the contributing key or a list of that contributor's inspected pages.

Contributions are linked through your invitation key. Error logs can record
your IP address; routine contribution requests are not logged.

Shared records have no automatic deletion period, and backups can retain earlier
copies. Turning contribution off stops uploads and clears waiting uploads;
already-shared data remains.

## Saved Checks

Saved checks may let someone with access to your browser profile work out which
pages were inspected. Passed decisions expire after 90 days and
excluded decisions after 180 days. These reuse periods also apply to shared
reports; expiry does not delete the server's stored records.

## Features Without Added Data Collection

- Local advertising, tracking, malware and category filtering.
- Single-domain and shopping-signal checks.
- Progress displays and show/hide controls.
- Search-engine switching links.
