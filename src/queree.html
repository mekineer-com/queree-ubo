uBO+Q

Search Filter & Content Blocker

uBlock Origin + Quer.ee (uBO+Q) is a modified version of uBlock Origin, maintained by Team GhostMaker. It keeps uBlock Origin's content blocking and adds Quer.ee filtering to Google and Bing results. We are not affiliated with or endorsed by the uBlock Origin project.

Filtering opens result websites in background tabs: disposable containers on desktop, reusable private tabs on Android. By default, Quer.ee removes desktop inspection-only visits from history when it can distinguish them from your own browsing.

Nothing is contributed to Quer.ee unless you save an invitation key and turn contribution on.

Site characteristics

Apply these conditions to whichever categories you include.

Loads resources only from its own domain. Links to other websites are fine.

Hide pages with ads, links marked as paid, or listed paid articles.
Hide pages with signals used to track visitors across unrelated websites.
Hide shopping-list matches, shopping-capable software or product-and-price offers.
Known prior and ordinary visits are kept; an indistinguishable simultaneous visit may also be removed.
Shares inspected addresses, page and result titles, eligible page text, result descriptions, single-domain and shopping observations, and exclusions with filter lists, rules and blocked hosts. To save time, the extension sends result links to Quer.ee and reuses recent checks from other contributors when available.
